Accent colour: six red-orange directions

Each direction replaces the cobalt accent and the light blue around it: the selected item, chart lines and bars, focus ring, sign-in sky. The near-night blues, the greys, fonts and layout are the same in every shot. Where an accent sits too close to the red of a failed test or the amber of a warning, that direction also moves the status colour, as shown on the failed run. The colours

Light: accent (selected text, focus), chart, selected-item tint, second chart line, bar track, stacked-bar ramp, sign-in sky. Dark: chart, menu tint, second chart line, chart fade and plan border, stacked-bar ramp. Status: danger and warning as each direction would ship them.

Next to failed and warning states

OKLCH distance from each accent to the danger and warning colours (hue in degrees, lightness 0 to 1, ΔE in OKLab). Too close means ΔE under 0.10, or hue within 14° and lightness within 0.12. A direction that is too close moves the status colour; the distance after the move is shown under it. Failed and warning states keep their icon and label everywhere.

Contrast

WCAG 2 ratios. Text needs 4.5:1, non-text UI 3:1. Tints are measured with the text that sits on them; the dark menu tints are blended at their opacity over the bar behind them.
